Essential Spanish Grammar Rule

Expressing Pain: "Me duele" vs "Me duelen"

In Spanish, you don't say "My head hurts" directly. Instead, you say "To me, the head is painful". Because the body part is the subject of the sentence, the verb matches whether the body part is singular or plural!

1. Singular Nouns
Me duele + singular noun

Examples: Me duele la cabeza (My head hurts), Me duele el brazo (My arm hurts).

2. Plural Nouns
Me duelen + plural noun

Examples: Me duelen las amígdalas (My tonsils hurt), Me duelen las pecas (My freckles hurt).
